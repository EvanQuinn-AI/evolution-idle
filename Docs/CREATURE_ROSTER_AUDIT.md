# Existing Creature Roster Audit

> June 2026 update: the browser progression roster now contains 41 forms. Eight of the bridge forms recommended below have been implemented in the prototype: Choanoflagellate, Jawless Fish, Lungfish, Synapsid, Feathered Theropod, Great Ape, Post-Human, and Voidborn. The original 33 IDs remain intact for save compatibility.

## Executive Assessment

The current 33-species roster is a good progression prototype but not yet a production visual roster. Its strongest quality is broad coverage from cellular life to space. Its main weakness is that many entries are taxonomic labels rather than designed characters.

The procedural prototype currently maps the roster onto broad forms such as particle, flora, swimmer, crawler, walker, and flyer. This is efficient for testing, but it causes different species to share body plans too aggressively. A reptile, mammal, wolf, primate, and human can all inherit the same walker foundation with feature flags. That cannot carry the final game's lineage fantasy.

## Immediate Priorities

1. Replace taxonomic placeholders with specific basal forms while preserving stable gameplay IDs where practical.
2. Design evolutionary transition pairs, not isolated species.
3. Add missing bridge forms before adding more terminal predators or spectacle creatures.
4. Separate organisms from societal/vehicle states after Human.
5. Use the Fish-to-Wolf lineage as the first production-quality visual proof.

## Strong Foundations

These entries already have clear silhouettes or strong gameplay fantasies:

- Bacteria: flagella and asymmetrical cell body provide a readable start.
- Sponge: distinct porous colony form and useful multicellular bridge.
- Mollusk: shell/soft-body contrast gives strong shape language.
- Insect: six-leg rhythm, antennae, and optional wings are highly readable.
- Shark: strong wedge silhouette and clear predator identity.
- Flying Fish: a recognizable mutation of Fish rather than a disconnected branch.
- Dinosaur: strong reveal potential if made specific enough.
- Bird: visually satisfying dinosaur inheritance opportunity.
- Wolf: personality, social behavior, and clear silhouette.
- Whale: excellent reversal of the land transition if mammalian anatomy remains visible.
- Primate: hands and posture provide an obvious innovation.
- Human: clear narrative milestone.
- Star Voyager: appropriate endpoint if represented as a lifeform, not only a vehicle.

## Weak or Over-Broad Designs

### Archaea and Extremophile

Problem: both risk reading as alternate colored bacteria. "Extremophile" is an ecological strategy, not a body plan.

Recommendation:

- Archaea inherits the cell silhouette but uses clustered membrane lobes and mineral-like surface plates.
- Extremophile becomes a named form such as a crystal-sheathed archaeon or dormant cyst colony.
- Differentiate through motion and survival posture, not spikes alone.

### Protist

Problem: too broad. It can become any single-celled eukaryote, which gives artists no silhouette target.

Recommendation: depict a ciliate-like hunter with an internal nucleus motif and a directional front. This establishes the face/locomotion axis inherited by early animals.

### Green Algae

Problem: currently treated like a generic swimmer despite being the plant-lineage root.

Recommendation: use a small linked colony that retains cellular lobes while introducing repeated chloroplast plates. Its descendants inherit repeated leaf/frond rhythms.

### Mold, Mushroom, and Parasitic Fungus

Problem: all are feature variations on the same flora form. Mold has no strong hero silhouette, and Parasitic Fungus depends visually on an absent host.

Recommendation:

- Mold becomes a branching mycelial colony with a visible pulse moving through the network.
- Mushroom inherits that branch rhythm into stem and cap gills.
- Parasitic Fungus must include a controlled host-shell silhouette or mobile spore carrier, while keeping the fungus as the readable subject.

### Worm and Arthropod

Problem: the jump from sponge to worm is useful, but Worm to Arthropod lacks a visible intermediate innovation.

Recommendation: make Worm a segmented annelid-like form. Arthropod visibly converts the same repeated segments into armored plates and jointed limb pairs.

### Fish

Problem: "Fish" is a placeholder immediately followed by highly specific Shark and Flying Fish forms.

Recommendation: redesign Fish as an early ray-finned generalist with a clear teal lateral stripe, paired fins, and simple rounded head. This becomes the parent motif for aquatic descendants.

### Amphibian

Problem: generic category and currently too close to a low crawler.

Recommendation: use a salamander-like basal amphibian. Retain the fish tail stripe and fin edge, then add four limbs, raised eyes, and wet skin. Avoid jumping directly to a modern frog silhouette.

### Reptile

Problem: generic category and currently distinguished mainly by tail and scale flags.

Recommendation: use a small early amniote silhouette. Keep the amphibian body length and tail accent, lower the moisture shine, add angular cheek plates, stronger toes, and a guarded brow.

### Dinosaur

Problem: one generic dinosaur cannot carry the visual distance between Reptile and Bird.

Recommendation: define it as a small feathering theropod rather than a giant mixed dinosaur. Large dinosaur branches can be added separately. The first dinosaur should make the Bird transition believable.

### Mammal

Problem: the current generic mammal is visually close to Wolf and does not communicate the nocturnal basal-mammal event.

Recommendation: use a small shrew-like basal mammal. Retain reptile tail/torso proportions, soften the jaw, add ear cups, fur clumps, a warm underbody, and faster breathing.

### Civilization, Spacefarer, and Star Voyager

Problem: these mix species, culture, and vehicles. A city or rocket is not a creature descendant and breaks the lineage presentation.

Recommendation:

- Keep Civilization as a progression state, not an animal species.
- Spacefarer should be the evolved humanoid wearing or integrating space adaptation.
- Star Voyager should be a post-human or engineered organism with inherited humanoid/primate cues and geometric luminous organs.
- Cities, rockets, and ships belong to world/environment presentation and unlock VFX.

## Redundancy Risks

- Bacteria, Archaea, and Extremophile are redundant unless membrane shape and motion differ strongly.
- Mold, Mushroom, Lichen, and Parasitic Fungus share too much flora language without a mycelium motif system.
- Reptile and Mammal are category placeholders that can be overshadowed by Dinosaur and Wolf.
- Fish, Shark, Flying Fish, and Whale need explicit inherited aquatic motifs to avoid becoming four unrelated swimmers.
- Primate and Human need more than upright posture; hands, shoulders, face plane, and social idle behavior must evolve visibly.

## Missing Evolutionary Bridges

The following gaps weaken visual continuity:

1. **Colonial cell or choanoflagellate-like form** between Protist and Sponge.
2. **Bilaterian softbody** between Sponge and Worm.
3. **Segmented armored transitional form** between Worm and Arthropod.
4. **Jawless or lobe-finned fish** before Amphibian.
5. **Early amniote** as the specific identity of Reptile.
6. **Feathered small theropod** between generic Dinosaur spectacle and Bird.
7. **Basal nocturnal mammal** as the specific identity of Mammal.
8. **Early ape or arboreal primate split** before Human.
9. **Post-human biological form** between Human and Spacefarer.
10. **Engineered vacuum-adapted life** before Star Voyager.

Not every bridge needs to launch as a separate gameplay node. Some may appear as the visual target of an existing broad ID, then become additional species in later content.

## Lineage Redesign Recommendations

### Origin to Animal

```text
Atom -> Molecule -> Organic -> Cell -> Bacteria -> Protist -> Sponge -> Worm
```

- Preserve the circular core from Atom through Cell.
- Convert orbital movement into organelle circulation in Cell and Bacteria.
- Give Protist a directional front and cilia.
- Repeat cell lobes as pores in Sponge.
- Stretch the sponge's bilateral axis into the segmented Worm.

### Marine to Land

```text
Fish -> Amphibian -> Reptile
```

- Keep one teal lateral accent through all three.
- Fish tail beat becomes Amphibian tail sway.
- Paired fins become limb positions.
- Amphibian wet highlight becomes Reptile plate edges.
- Eye position rises gradually and brow angle becomes more guarded.

### Reptile Split

```text
Reptile -> Small Theropod -> Bird
        -> Basal Mammal -> Wolf / Whale / Primate
```

- Reptile supplies the long tail, angular jaw, and amber plate accent.
- Theropod shifts mass over the hips and turns plate edges into feather fans.
- Bird keeps the hip/foot language and tail accent while expanding feathers.
- Basal Mammal rounds the jaw, adds ears/fur, and warms the palette without losing the long torso.

### Mammal Branches

- Wolf exaggerates basal-mammal ears, muzzle, legs, and social eye direction.
- Whale keeps mammalian jaw, ribcage, warm underbody, and breathing behavior while limbs return to flippers.
- Primate shifts emphasis from muzzle to hands, shoulders, and forward eyes.
- Human reduces body hair but keeps the primate hand, shoulder, face, and social gesture language.

### Future Branch

- Post-human forms keep the human hand or face proportion as an ancestry cue.
- Emission follows existing lineage accents rather than replacing them.
- Floating pieces should align with ancestral joints or organs.
- Technology may merge with anatomy, but vehicles never substitute for the creature.

## Current Prototype Inconsistencies

The browser prototype is intentionally schematic, but these limitations should not be copied into Unity production:

- Group palette is doing too much of the identification work.
- Generic `walker` covers reptile, mammal, wolf, primate, and human.
- Generic `flora` covers plants, fungi, and sponge.
- Creature scale is partly derived from evolution cost, which conflates progression value with anatomy.
- Feature flags add appendages but do not preserve inherited proportions.
- Civilization and space forms replace organisms with city/rocket/UFO archetypes.
- Population silhouettes reuse the active creature color rather than representing ecosystem diversity.

The prototype should remain a gameplay renderer. It should consume future visual-definition data where useful, but it is not the source of truth for final asset anatomy.

## Vertical Slice Acceptance Criteria

The first five-form slice is approved only if:

- Fish, Amphibian, Reptile, Mammal, and Wolf read correctly in black silhouette.
- Reviewers can order the five forms without labels.
- Each child visibly inherits at least three motifs from its parent.
- All forms fit the defined rig-family and material architecture.
- LOD1 remains readable on a 390 x 844 viewport.
- The complete scene meets target frame time on a representative low-tier Android device.
- High and low Karma states alter presentation without changing species identity.
- No bespoke material instance or species-specific animator controller is required.
